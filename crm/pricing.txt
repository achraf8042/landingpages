B. THE SOLOPRENEUR'S PAIN POINTS WITH EXISTING EMRs:
1. Perpetual Expense: Over 5 years, an independent doctor pays between 
   $7,500 and $25,000+ simply for permission to access their own software.
2. Data Hostage Dilemma: If a doctor stops paying the monthly fee, their 
   account is locked. Extracting patient files often incurs thousands in 
   "export and migration" fees.
3. Cloud Downtime & Internet Dependency: An internet outage or cloud 
   server disruption brings the entire clinic to a halt.
4. Privacy & Compliance Exposure: Cloud breaches, third-party server 
   surveillance, and mandatory telemetry expose sensitive medical data.
