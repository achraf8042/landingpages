PRODUCT OVERVIEW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DigiSpher EMR 2.0 is a sovereign, offline-first Electronic Medical Record 
(EMR) and Practice Management Suite designed specifically for independent 
physicians, solo medical practitioners, and private clinics. 

Unlike bloated cloud platforms that charge recurring monthly subscription 
fees and store your sensitive patient data on external third-party servers, 
DigiSpher EMR gives you 100% data ownership, blazing-fast native Windows 
performance, and a comprehensive clinical command center with ZERO recurring 
fees.

From clinical SOAP documentation and multi-drug prescriptions to patient 
billing, practice expenses, and daily revenue intelligence, DigiSpher EMR 
delivers every tool needed to manage a modern, profitable medical practice 
in a clean, distraction-free interface.

DETAILED FEATURE BREAKDOWN BY MODULE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD & PRACTICE COMMAND DOCK
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your morning clinical cockpit. Open the app and immediately know the pulse 
of your clinic:
• Real-Time Practice KPIs:
  - Total Active Patients
  - Today's Documented Consultations
  - Today's Collected Revenue
  - Today's Scheduled Visits
  - This Week's Total Consultations
• Clinical Pulse Panel: Live activity feed showing the most recent clinical 
  consultations, active prescriptions, and diagnostic summaries with 1-click 
  navigation into the patient chart.
• Financial Pulse Panel: Real-time billing ledger displaying recent invoices, 
  billed amounts, patient names, and color-coded payment status badges 
  (PAID, UNPAID, PARTIAL).
• Solopreneur Practice Command Dock: A high-utility 1-click shortcut dock 
  spanning the base of the dashboard:
  - [ + New Patient ]: Instant chart registration
  - [ + Book Visit ]: Direct calendar scheduling
  - [ + Clinical Note ]: Immediate SOAP consultation entry
  - [ + Prescribe Rx ]: Multi-drug prescription generator
  - [ + Issue Invoice ]: Patient billing and itemized service receipts
  - [ Practice Finances ]: One-click financial P&L and tax export
• Privacy Mode Toggle: One-click doctor privacy switch that masks patient 
  surnames on the dashboard and lists (e.g., "J. D.") during screen sharing 
  or in the presence of visitors.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. COMPREHENSIVE PATIENT 360° RECORDS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
A centralized, organized electronic chart for every patient:
• Demographics & Identity: Full name, date of birth, gender, blood group, 
  phone number, email, complete address, and national health ID.
• Emergency Contacts: Direct relative name, relationship, and emergency phone.
• Critical Allergy Warning Hub: Prominently tracks active allergens, allergic 
  reaction profiles, and clinical severity ratings (Mild, Moderate, Severe). 
  Severe allergies trigger high-visibility alerts during clinical workflows.
• Vital Signs Chronology: Detailed tracking of Systolic/Diastolic Blood 
  Pressure, Heart Rate, Body Temperature, Weight, Height, BMI (auto-computed), 
  Oxygen Saturation (SpO2), and Respiratory Rate.
• Complete Longitudinal History: All historical consultations, SOAP notes, 
  prescriptions, appointments, and billing invoices grouped under a single 
  patient view.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CLINICAL SOAP NOTES & MEDICAL TEMPLATES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fast, structured medical record keeping complying with international clinical 
standards:
• Standardized SOAP Framework:
  - Subjective: Chief complaint, history of present illness (HPI), symptoms.
  - Objective: Physical examination findings, vitals, lab results.
  - Assessment: Primary diagnosis, differential diagnosis, ICD coding notes.
  - Plan: Medical management, treatment plan, lifestyle guidance, follow-up.
• Instant Clinical Templates: Pre-configured templates for common clinical 
  scenarios (General Consult, Annual Physical, Follow-up Visit, SOAP Standard).
• Consultation Status Management: Work in "DRAFT" mode and lock to "FINALIZED" 
  with timestamping to maintain medico-legal integrity.
• Search & Filter: Instant search by patient name, consultation date, or 
  diagnostic keywords.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MULTI-MEDICATION PRESCRIPTIONS & A5 PDF GENERATOR
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Issue professional, clear, error-free prescriptions in seconds:
• Multi-Drug Prescribing: Add unlimited medications per prescription encounter:
  - Medication Name & Brand
  - Exact Dosage (e.g., 500mg, 10ml, 1 tablet)
  - Administration Frequency (e.g., 3x daily after meals, BID, PRN)
  - Treatment Duration (e.g., 7 days, 1 month, ongoing)
• Real-Time Allergy Safety Alert: Selecting a patient instantly cross-checks 
  their documented allergies and displays an active warning banner if the 
  patient has known drug hypersensitivities.
• Professional A5 Print-Ready PDF Generation:
  - Automatically compiles an elegant, internationally formatted A5 
    prescription document via ReportLab.
  - Features clinic header, doctor license credentials, contact details.
  - Clean medication table with clear dosage instructions.
  - Special patient instructions section and doctor signature block.
  - Direct 1-click printing or PDF export for patient sharing.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PRACTICE FINANCES, BILLING & CASH FLOW INTELLIGENCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
A complete clinic financial management module tailored for medical solo 
practices—no third-party accounting software required:
• Invoicing & Billing:
  - Create itemized patient invoices for consultations, procedures, and tests.
  - Dynamic service line items with automatic subtotal and customizable tax.
  - Payment tracking: Mark as UNPAID, PARTIAL, or PAID with 1-click.
  - Generate clean, professional Invoice / Receipt PDFs for patients.
• Practice Expense Tracking:
  - Log operational clinic overhead across standard categories (Rent, Medical 
    Supplies, Utilities, Software, Equipment, Staff, Other).
  - Track dates, descriptions, and payment amounts for tax write-offs.
• Cash Flow & P&L Intelligence:
  - Real-time KPI summary: Today's Revenue, Month-to-Date Revenue, Month-to-Date 
    Expenses, Net Profit (Green for surplus, Red for deficit), and Pending Balances.
  - Visual Financial Trends: Interactive native daily cash flow bar charts 
    comparing everyday revenue vs. operational costs.
• Accountant CSV Export: 
  - One-click export of all invoices and expenses into standard CSV format, 
    ready for your CPA or tax accountant.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CALENDAR & APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eliminate scheduling conflicts and missed consultations:
• Scheduled Visits Manager: Track patient name, appointment date/time, doctor, 
  reason for visit, and visit status (SCHEDULED, CONFIRMED, IN_PROGRESS, 
  COMPLETED, CANCELLED, NO_SHOW).
• Smart Filtering: View today's appointments, this week's visits, or monthly 
  schedules at a glance.
• High-Contrast Interactive Calendar Picker:
  - Custom zero-padding calendar widget ensuring every single date (1 to 31) 
    is 100% visible, centered, and razor-sharp.
  - High-contrast day headers, soft indigo selection highlighting, and fast 
    month/year navigation.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PRIVACY, SECURITY & DATA SOVEREIGNTY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Medical software must be private by design:
• 100% Local SQLite Architecture: All patient data, clinical notes, and billing 
  records reside exclusively on your local computer.
• SQLite WAL Mode (Write-Ahead Logging): Provides high-concurrency read/write 
  speed, zero application lock-ups, and instant data persistence.
• Automated Rotating Exit Backups: Every time you close DigiSpher EMR, the 
  system automatically creates a timestamped database backup in your secure 
  user directory, maintaining a rolling 7-day recovery archive.
• Session Inactivity Security: Configurable auto-logout timer (5 to 60 minutes) 
  protects patient charts when you step away from your desk.
• Password Protection & Doctor Profile: Secure bcrypt-grade password hashing 
  with forced password change upon first login.
• Windows AppUserModelID Taskbar Grouping: Integrates cleanly into Windows with 
  its own standalone taskbar icon, never grouped under Python or generic executables.
