                          FIRST-TIME SETUP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Thank you for installing DigiSpher EMR! Follow these steps to get started:

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. DEFAULT LOGIN CREDENTIALS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Username: doctor
   Password: doctor123

   ⚠ IMPORTANT: You will be REQUIRED to change this password on first login!

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. INITIAL SETUP PROCESS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Step 1: Launch DigiSpher EMR
   ═══════════════════════════════
   - Double-click the DigiSpherEMR.exe icon
   - The application will initialize your database automatically

   Step 2: First Login
   ═══════════════════════════════
   - Enter the default username: doctor
   - Enter the default password: doctor123
   - Click "Login"

   Step 3: Clinic Information Setup
   ═══════════════════════════════
   - Choose English or Français in the Language selector.
   - The language can be changed later in Settings > Language.
   - You will be prompted to enter your clinic information:
     • Clinic Name (required)
     • Clinic Address (required)
     • License Number (required)
   - Fill in all fields and click "Save & Continue"

   Step 4: Complete Your Profile
   ═══════════════════════════════
   - Update your personal information:
     • First Name
     • Last Name
     • Speciality (e.g., General Practitioner, Cardiologist)
     • Doctor Title (e.g., MD, DO, MBBS)
   - Set a new secure password (REQUIRED)
   - Click "Save & Continue to Dashboard"

   Step 5: Start Using DigiSpher EMR
   ═══════════════════════════════
   - You're all set! You can now:
     • Add patients
     • Schedule appointments
     • Create clinical notes
     • Write prescriptions
     • Manage your practice

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. QUICK START GUIDE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Dashboard
   ═══════════
   - View today's appointments
   - See patient statistics
   - Quick access to common actions

   Patients
   ═══════════
   - Click "Add Patient" to register new patients
   - Search and view patient records
   - Track allergies and vital signs

   Appointments
   ═══════════
   - Click "New Appointment" to schedule
   - Filter by Day/Week/Month
   - Update appointment status

   Clinical Notes
   ═══════════
   - Document patient visits using SOAP format
   - Link to appointments
   - Export to PDF

   Prescriptions
   ═══════════
   - Write prescriptions for patients
   - Track medication history
   - Generate prescription PDFs

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. IMPORTANT INFORMATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   ⚠ Data Storage
   ═══════════════
   DigiSpher EMR is an ON-PREMISE solution. All your data is stored locally:
   - Windows: C:\Users\[YourUsername]\AppData\Roaming\DigiSpher EMR\
   - Database file: data\clinic.db
   - Log files: logs\clinic_emr.log

   💾 Backup Your Data
   ═══════════════════
   CRITICAL: You are responsible for backing up your data regularly!
   - Go to Settings > Database Management > Backup Database
   - Store backups in a safe location
   - Consider daily/weekly backup schedules

   🔒 Security Best Practices
   ═══════════════════════════
   - Use a strong password (8+ characters, mix of letters/numbers/symbols)
   - Change your password regularly
   - Lock your computer when away
   - Restrict physical access to the machine
   - Keep the application updated

   ⏱ Auto-Logout
   ═══════════════
   - The system auto-logs out after 15 minutes of inactivity (default)
   - Adjust this in Settings > Security > Inactivity Timeout

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. PASSWORD RECOVERY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   The clinic name is not a password recovery credential. If you forget your
   password, contact your clinic administrator. There is no built-in
   self-service reset; keep a protected copy of your credentials and backups.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Minimum Requirements:
   - Operating System: Windows 10 or later
   - RAM: 4 GB
   - Disk Space: 500 MB free space
   - Display: 1280 x 720 minimum resolution

   Recommended:
   - RAM: 8 GB or more
   - SSD for better performance
   - Display: 1920 x 1080 or higher
